Container Survey Services

Professional container condition assessment for better operational decisions.

A container survey provides a structured assessment of a shipping container's visible condition, damage and relevant equipment components. Swamy Marine Services (OPC) Private Limited supports clients who require reliable container condition information before accepting, returning, moving or continuing to operate container equipment.

Our container survey services cover Navi Mumbai, Nhava Sheva (JNPT), Dighi Port and other major locations across India, subject to operational requirements and assignment scope.

Survey Types

Container surveys for different stages of container operations.

01

On-Hire Container Survey

Condition assessment before a container is accepted or placed into service. The survey can document existing dents, repairs, floor condition, doors, locking gear, CSC information, cleanliness and visible defects.

02

Off-Hire Container Survey

Return-stage condition assessment to document the container's condition, visible damage and repair requirements when equipment is returned.

03

Container Condition Survey

Detailed visual assessment of the container structure and components, including roof, walls, floor, doors, locking gear and other visible condition points.

04

Container Damage Survey

Documentation of visible dents, deformation, corrosion, holes, structural concerns and other damage observations.

05

CSC Plate Verification

Verification and documentation of container identification and visible CSC plate information during the survey process.

06

Photo-Backed Survey Reporting

Photographic documentation can support condition observations and provide useful evidence for operational and commercial communication.

Survey Process

A systematic approach to container condition assessment.

01

Container Identification

Verify container number and available equipment information before beginning the inspection.

02

External Inspection

Inspect visible external areas including side walls, front wall, roof, frame and other accessible structural components.

03

Internal Inspection

Review accessible internal areas, including floor condition, cleanliness, visible defects, light leaks and other observations.

04

Doors & Locking Gear

Check doors, hinges, locking bars, handles, gaskets and related visible components.

05

Damage Documentation

Record visible damage, corrosion, deformation and other relevant condition observations.

06

Survey Report

Compile observations and photographic information into clear survey documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Container Survey FAQs

What is a container survey?

A container survey is a systematic inspection and condition assessment of a shipping container to document visible condition, damage, structural components and operational suitability.

What does a container survey include?

Depending on the assignment, a container survey can include container number verification, CSC plate inspection, external and internal condition checks, roof and side wall inspection, floor inspection, doors, locking gear, damage assessment and photographic reporting.

Do you provide container surveys at JNPT and Nhava Sheva?

Yes. Swamy Marine Services provides container survey and inspection support around Navi Mumbai, JNPT, Nhava Sheva and Dighi Port, as well as other locations across India depending on the assignment.

Do you provide on-hire and off-hire container surveys?

Yes. On-hire, off-hire, condition assessment and damage survey support can be provided according to the specific inspection requirement.
