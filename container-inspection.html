Container Inspection

Container inspection before problems become operational costs.

Swamy Marine Services (OPC) Private Limited provides container inspection services designed to identify visible damage, structural concerns, equipment condition and repair requirements. Our inspection approach helps shipping and logistics teams make informed decisions before containers are accepted, moved, loaded or returned.

Inspection Scope

What we inspect

01

Container Identification

Container number, identification markings and CSC plate information are checked and documented.

02

External Structure

Side walls, front wall, roof and visible structural areas are examined for damage and deformation.

03

Container Floor

The internal floor condition is reviewed for damage, deterioration, contamination and visible defects.

04

Doors & Locking Gear

Doors, hinges, handles, locking bars, gaskets and related components are visually assessed.

05

Corrosion & Damage

Visible corrosion, dents, cracks, holes, deformation and other condition concerns are recorded.

06

Inspection Reporting

Observations are organized into clear documentation supported by photographs where required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Container Inspection FAQs

What is container inspection?

Container inspection is a systematic visual assessment of a shipping container to identify its condition, visible damage, structural concerns and operational issues.

What is checked during container inspection?

Inspection can include container identification, CSC plate, external structure, roof, side walls, doors, locking gear, floor condition, corrosion and visible damage.

Do you provide container inspection at JNPT and Nhava Sheva?

Swamy Marine Services provides container survey and inspection support in Navi Mumbai, JNPT, Nhava Sheva, Dighi Port and other locations across India.
